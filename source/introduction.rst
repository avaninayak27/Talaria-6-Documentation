Introduction
============

Talaria-6 is a highly integrated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and BLE 5.0 SoC. It is designed to enable cloud-connected battery-operated devices that were previously impossible due to power constraints.

Applications
------------
* Smart Doorlocks
* Medical Wearables
* Industrial Sensors
